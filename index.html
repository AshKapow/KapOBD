About

KapOBDLive data and fault codes for your car, in the browser.

Disclaimer. The app reads standard diagnostic data and is not a substitute for professional diagnosis. Clearing codes does not repair faults. Do not interact with the app while driving.

Version . Connects to an ELM327-compatible Bluetooth LE adapter (tested target: Veepeak OBDCheck BLE+). On iPhone and iPad it needs the Bluefy browser, because Safari has no Web Bluetooth.

It only ever sends adapter (AT) commands and the read-only OBD-II modes 01, 02, 03, 06, 07, 09 and 0A. Mode 04 (clear codes) is only sent from the Codes screen after warnings, an engine-off check and two confirmations, and is never retried automatically.

Vehicle profiles, settings and the last session’s log are kept on this device only.
